### Trigger-snapshot reachability ruling

The closed event vocabulary can replace the stable pointer while an older trigger remains open and
can advance feedback after a reflector starts. The stale-snapshot test varies both reachable facts.
No public event changes `learning_epoch`, so its trigger-to-current comparison remains a defensive
predicate and this map does not claim an independently killable epoch mutant.

### Defensive-CAS reachability ruling

The reducer implements every M1/M2 promotion and rollback predicate, but the closed public event
vocabulary cannot vary all of them independently:

- Promotion job status comes only from `initial_state`; a nonrepeatable or cancelled job cannot
  create the trigger/candidate/trial needed to reach promotion.
- Trial job/digest, candidate/replacement/base/epoch/algorithm/source-manifest, compatibility,
  adapter/gate, and settled-cohort facts derive from the validated candidate/admission/trial chain.
  `initial_state` rejects derived trial state, and no event rewrites those facts. Candidate controls
  also advance feedback and reconcile the trial, so candidate status cannot miss independently.
- No open-trial event changes the stable pointer, learning epoch, algorithm, job definition, or
  compatibility. Feedback revision is the one independently mutable promotion predicate and is
  covered directly.
- The retained rollback promotion/candidate facts, promotion digest, promotion-revision formula,
  epoch, algorithm, job definition, and compatibility are reducer-derived and immutable;
  `initial_state` also rejects derived promotion state. Promotion revision is not an event field.
- After activation, promotion status, stable digest, and stable revision move only through a
  successful promotion or rollback. A repeated rollback covers their reachable combined miss, but
  no public sequence changes only one of those three values.
- Successful rollback atomically closes any successor trial frozen to the replaced pointer. No
  public event can therefore reach `trial_run_created` with an open stale-base trial; its base
  predicate remains a defensive guard. The dependent-trial rollback test proves the reachable
  closure and then observes `policy.no_open_trial` without fabricating derived state.

The tests additionally cover the promotion/candidate/replacement identities independently supplied
by rollback receipts and successful exact CAS. Separate tests for the remaining defensive predicates
would require fabricated derived state and would not name a reachable mutant.
